Key Research Findings

🏆 SVM Outperforms Traditional ML

SVM with RBF kernel achieved 87% accuracy vs Logistic Regression's 42.7%, showing that non-linear boundaries are critical for audio classification.

🔊 Acoustic Similarity Drives Errors

The most confused pairs — street music vs children playing — share overlapping frequency ranges and irregular temporal patterns, causing systematic misclassification.

📊 Class Imbalance Affects Results

Gun shot (374 clips) and car horn (429 clips) had significantly fewer samples than other classes, impacting model reliability on rare events.

🎯 MFCCs Are Powerful Features

40 MFCC coefficients captured enough audio texture for 87% accuracy, validating their use as the gold standard feature for urban sound classification.
