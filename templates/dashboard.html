Key Research Deductions (For Journal Discussion)

1. Non-Linear Feature Spaces Dominate

Logistic Regression achieved only 58.0% compared to SVM (86.7%) and Random Forest (89.6%), confirming that 1D MFCC acoustic features cannot be separated by linear hyperplanes.

2. Stationary vs. Transient Acoustic Signatures

Continuous sounds (engine idling, air conditioner) achieve >92% F1 with 1D statistical MFCC averages. However, impulsive transient sounds (gun shot, dog bark) benefit substantially from 2D time-frequency spectrogram convolutions.

3. Late-Fusion Superiority (Research Contribution)

The proposed Hybrid Late-Fusion ensemble (92.1% F1) surpasses both standalone classical and deep models by leveraging the complementary strengths of 1D spectral summaries, temporal sequential frames, and 2D local spectrogram features.

4. Low Computational Footprint for Edge Deployment

Classical models require only ~8–14 ms inference latency per clip, rendering them viable for low-power microcontrollers and real-time edge acoustic monitoring.
